- 2026-09-24: Added `FilterSpec.py` to turn a ranked stack into dichroic and band-pass filter specs. `Plot_Stack` gains `selFile=` (writes the exact selected lines of the top `nTop` ranks, with scores and the xlsx/h5 folder used, before any figure opens) and `plot=False`; its float16-to-exact lookup now only accepts a match within half a float16 step and falls back to the full spreadsheet, so stacks graded before a line-list change resolve to the line actually graded instead of a neighbour. `FilterSpec` reuses the grading catalog (`Merge_Zeeman_Catalog`, new `returnLabels=`; `Line_Envelopes`, new `returnIdx=`): each dichroic window runs from the red edge of the lower line's envelope to the blue edge of the upper one's; each band-pass gets its own envelope, any blends, the nearest modelled radiation on each side, and pass/block limits with optional CWL tolerance and AOI blue shift (Macleod).
